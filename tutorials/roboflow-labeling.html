Roboflow Labeling Setup

Two steps to turn on Roboflow auto-labeling: make an account, then fork the labeling workflow into it.

Why fork a copy? Roboflow has no way to let you call a workflow you don't own on your own credits. Forking gives you your own copy — your labeling usage runs on your own free account, not the developer's.
Free plan credits are limited, monthly. The free Public Plan includes a set amount of credits per month (15/month, as observed setting this up — Roboflow may change this over time). Once used up, auto-labeling stops working until they refill next cycle — or you upgrade to a paid plan. Manual labeling in Screen Macro always still works either way.
1

Create a Roboflow account

Click Create account in Screen Macro's Settings tab, or go to app.roboflow.com/login directly. Pick whichever sign-in method is easiest — Google, Github, email, or SSO all work the same for this.

Roboflow then shows the account it found (name/email) and asks you to accept its Terms of Service — check the box and click Continue to finish creating the account.

Next it asks you to name a workspace and pick a plan. Public Plan is the always-free tier (the one the credits note above applies to). Premium Trial is a 14-day trial of the paid tier, no card required — fine to try, it drops back to free-tier limits on its own after 14 days.

After that you'll land on a "what are you building?" home screen — that's for building something from scratch with Roboflow's own agent. You don't need it, just skip it.

From there you end up on your workspace dashboard. Account creation is now done.

2

Fork the labeling workflow

Click Fork the labeling workflow… in Screen Macro's Settings tab. It opens the shared SAM 3 workflow below — this can take a moment to load.

  1. Click the purple Fork this workflow button, bottom-left.
  2. A "Create Workflow" confirmation opens, previewing the same pipeline — click Create Workflow.
  1. Roboflow copies it into your own workspace and opens your copy.
  2. Click Publish, top-right of the editor — a fork only runs once it's published.
  3. Click </> Use, right next to Publish (greyed out until published).

Copy ENDPOINT as one string — it looks like https://serverless.roboflow.com/your-workspace/workflows/your-workflow. Click the eye icon to reveal API KEY, then copy it too.

Back in Screen Macro

Paste your API key into Roboflow API key, and the endpoint URL into Roboflow endpoint URL, in the Settings tab's Labeling group. Auto-labeling suggestions turn on the next time you use Label Frames.
